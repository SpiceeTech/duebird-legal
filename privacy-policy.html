Duebird Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Duebird ("we", "us") is an app for keeping track of bills, subscriptions and warranties, operated by SPICEETECH (PTY) LTD ("SpiceeTech"). This policy explains what data Duebird handles, where it goes, and how you can delete it.

Your bills, warranties and photos stay on your phone

Duebird has no accounts and no cloud sync. Everything you enter (bills, subscriptions, warranties, amounts, dates, notes, and receipt and product photos) is stored only on your device. We never receive it and cannot see it.

  • Photos: if you attach a photo, Duebird asks for camera or photo library access only to take or pick that photo. The copy is kept inside the app's private storage.
  • Reminders: reminders are scheduled on your phone as local notifications. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.
  • Backups: when you export a backup, Duebird creates a file and you choose where it goes (for example your Downloads folder or Google Drive). That file is then under your control and that service's terms. We never receive a copy.

Ads

The free version of Duebird shows banner ads from Google AdMob on the Bills and Warranties screens. To show and measure ads, Google's ads software on your phone collects and sends to Google information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and operating system, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. Your bills, warranties and photos are never shared with Google or any advertiser.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Duebird asks for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads on most Android phones). How Google uses this data is described in Google's privacy information for partner apps.

Remove Ads purchase

You can remove ads with a one-time purchase. Google Play takes the payment under its own terms. We never see or store your card or bank details. RevenueCat, our purchase service provider, confirms the purchase with Google Play so the app knows ads are removed. To do this it uses a random identifier created by the app (not your name or email), the purchase record, and basic device details such as country and app version. Records of purchases are kept for accounting and legal purposes.

Who we share data with

We don't sell your data. The only service providers involved are Google (AdMob ads, and Google Play for payments) and RevenueCat (confirming purchases), as described above. Duebird does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Deleting your data

You can delete any bill, warranty or photo in the app. Uninstalling Duebird permanently deletes everything it stored on your phone, so export a backup first if you want to keep your records. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected. Delete them where you saved them. To clear the data Google uses for ads, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. To ask about purchase records, contact us below.

Children's privacy

Duebird is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act and the EU and UK GDPR), you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to how it is used. Because your records stay on your device, we hold very little about you: at most a purchase record. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, let you know in the app.
